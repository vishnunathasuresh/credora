## Design Context

### Users

Credora serves credential holders, issuing organizations, recruiters and
verifiers, and system administrators. The primary jobs are issuing a trusted
credential, carrying it across organizations, and checking it quickly without
requiring blockchain expertise.

### Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, modern. The experience should reduce uncertainty around
credential authenticity and make decentralized infrastructure feel simple,
legible, and dependable.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a composed, editorial interface with a warm paper-like light theme and a
deep ink-like dark theme. Favor strong typography, clear provenance cues,
quiet color accents, and generous whitespace over crypto-market neon, noisy
gradients, or dashboard clutter.

### Design Principles

1. Make trust visible: show issuer, timestamp, verification state, and source
   without exposing implementation jargon.
2. Keep the primary action obvious: issue, verify, share, or view should be
   clear before secondary details appear.
3. Treat failure states as useful status: distinguish unavailable, pending,
   rejected, and invalid instead of collapsing them into one error.
4. Use restrained color and motion to communicate state, not decoration.
5. Preserve accessibility across light/dark themes, reduced motion, keyboard
   navigation, and mobile touch targets.
